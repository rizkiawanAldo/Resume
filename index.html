02. Mindset & Journey

From Physical Signals to Data Streams

Seismic Wave Modeling → Real-Time Microservice Message Streams

I'm Aldo Rizkiawan. My engineering path began in Geophysics Engineering at ITS Surabaya, where I spent years analyzing wave propagation, modeling earth systems, and decoding signal noise under uncertainty.

That background gave me a distinct edge in software: I treat large distributed backend architectures as complex physical systems where latency, signal loss, and concurrency race conditions must be carefully bounded.

Over the past 7 years, I've built real-time fraud engines at Tokopedia (saving ~120M IDR/month), streamlined document automation at EximBank, and launched insurance & developer documentation platforms at Grab.

When I'm not writing Golang or C#, I'm shooting 35mm film photography through Xenosumedia and coding privacy-first browser utilities.
